# Mundial Vault — Marketing Kit

Everything here is ready to copy-paste. Budget assumption: $0–$300/month to
start. Organic first, paid only after the first organic sales prove the offer.

## Positioning

**One-liner:** Retro football style. No logos, all love.

**Who it's for:** 18–35 football fans in the US who grew up on
YouTube compilations of the classics, want the retro look without paying
$90 for official merch, and buy from TikTok/Instagram.

**Why now:** the World Cup just ended. Fans are in the afterglow — winner's
celebration content, "best kits of the tournament" nostalgia, and watch-party
habits that carry into the new club season in August.

## Phase 1 — Launch week (organic, $0)

1. Create TikTok + Instagram as **@mundialvault** (same handle, same bio:
   "Retro football style. No logos, all love. 🌍 Free ship over $50").
2. Post 1x/day using the content formats below. Film on a phone; product
   on a plain background or on you.
3. Put the site link in bio. Every video ends with "link in bio".
4. Post the retro shirts to r/vintage_soccer-adjacent subreddits and football
   Discords **as a fan, not an ad** — "made a logo-free tribute to the '70
   kit, thoughts?" works; spam gets banned.

## Content formats that work in this niche

| Format | Example hook | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Nostalgia rank | "Ranking the 5 greatest World Cup kits ever (no.1 is not what you think)" | Comment bait, no product needed |
| Reveal | "POV: you want the retro look but not the $90 price tag" → shirt reveal | Direct product demo |
| Problem/solution | "They wouldn't let her into the stadium because of her bag…" → clear bag | Stadium bag sells itself |
| Custom tease | "Comment your country and I'll show you its colors on our tee" | Engagement engine for the 48-nation tee |
| Watch party | "How to host a Champions League watch party for under $30" | Bundles the party kit |

## 30-day content calendar (repeatable)

- **Mon:** Nostalgia/ranking video (top kits, iconic moments — no product)
- **Tue:** Product reveal (rotate: retro shirt → scarf → hoodie)
- **Wed:** Customer-style content (order packing, "first 10 orders" story)
- **Thu:** Custom nation tee engagement post ("comment your country")
- **Fri:** Problem/solution (stadium bag or party kit)
- **Sat:** Watch-party / matchday lifestyle content
- **Sun:** Behind-the-scenes (picking designs, polls: "which colorway next?")

Repeat weekly. After 30 days, double down on the two formats with the best
views-to-profile-visits ratio.

## Ad copy (for when you turn on paid — Phase 2)

Only start paid ads after ~5 organic sales. Start with $10/day on Meta
(Instagram Reels placement), advantage+ audience, US, 18–35, interest:
soccer. Kill any ad under 1% CTR after $30 spend.

**Ad 1 — Retro shirt (video: slow reveal)**
> The greatest kits ever made… without the logo markup.
> Retro-inspired shirts from $34.99. Free US shipping over $50.
> 30-day fit guarantee. Tap to shop the collection.

**Ad 2 — Nation tee (video: 48 flags flashing)**
> 48 nations. Your name on the back. Your colors.
> Custom nation tees $24.99 — link in bio.

**Ad 3 — Stadium bag (video: gate-denial skit)**
> Don't get turned away at the gate.
> Stadium-approved clear bag, $19.99. Ships tracked.

## Email (start from order #1)

Collect every buyer's email. Three automated emails (Mailchimp free tier):
1. **Order confirmation + story** — "You're order #12. Here's why we make
   logo-free kits…" (builds the brand, reduces refunds)
2. **Shipping/tracking** — set delivery expectations honestly (7–14 days)
3. **Post-delivery +5 days** — ask for a photo in the shirt (repost = free
   content) + 10% code for a scarf/flag add-on

## SEO (already partly done)

- Site meta tags target: *retro football shirt, nation colors tee, clear
  stadium bag, watch party kit* ✅ (in `index.html`)
- Next: register a custom domain (mundialvault.com) — GitHub Pages supports
  it free; Pages URLs rank poorly and look untrustworthy in ads
- Later: one blog-style page per retro era ("The story of the '70 gold kit")
  — these rank for nostalgia searches and feed the product pages

## KPIs — check weekly in the Dashboard tab

| Metric | Target (month 1) |
|---|---|
| TikTok/IG posts | 25+ |
| Profile → site clicks | 200+ |
| Email-order conversions | 5–10 sales |
| Average order value | ≥ $45 (push bundles) |
| Gross margin after ads | ≥ 30% |

If month 1 hits 10 sales: turn on Stripe links, start $10/day ads, and move
checkout to Shopify. If not: change the products (the site makes that a
5-minute edit in `products.js`), not the strategy.
